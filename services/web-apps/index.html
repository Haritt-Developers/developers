Service 02 · Web apps & dashboards

Web apps and dashboards for the work your team does every day.

When enquiries, orders and tasks live in WhatsApp chats, spreadsheets and memory, things slip. We map how your business actually runs, then build software around it.

Asked before projects like this.

Do we have to change how we work?+
As little as possible. We design the system around your current process and fix only the parts that cause problems.
Can it connect to tools we already use?+
Usually, yes. We check what your current tools allow before we promise an integration.
Where is our data stored?+
We explain where the data will be stored and who can access it before we build, in plain words.
