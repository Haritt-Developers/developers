Service 04 · E-commerce

Online stores where browsing, paying and delivery just work.

Selling online is more than a product page. We set up the store, payments, stock and delivery updates together, so customers can buy in a few taps and you are not chasing orders by hand.

01 · Signs you need this

If any of these sound familiar.

01

You sell on Instagram or WhatsApp and send prices by hand.

What changesA catalogue with prices that customers can browse and order from.

02

Customers drop off at checkout.

What changesFewer steps, UPI and COD, and clear delivery dates.

03

Stock is counted in a notebook.

What changesStock updates with every order.

04

Customers ask where their order is.

What changesAutomatic updates on WhatsApp or email at each step.

03 · Two ways to explain it

For the owner, and for the tech team.

In plain words

An online shop that looks trustworthy, takes payments safely and keeps track of orders and stock for you.

Under the hood

Hosted platform or custom storefront depending on catalogue size, payment gateway integration, inventory sync, order webhooks, transactional messages, product schema for search.

05 · Questions

Asked before projects like this.

Shopify or custom?+
It depends on your catalogue, budget and how you sell. We recommend one after understanding your business, and explain why.
Can I keep selling on WhatsApp and Instagram?+
Yes. We connect those channels to the store so orders end up in one place.
Where does the money from sales go?+
The payment gateway account is opened for your business, so payments from customers go to your account.
