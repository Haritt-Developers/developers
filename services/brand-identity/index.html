Service 06 · Brand & digital identity

A brand identity that looks the same everywhere it appears.

Customers notice when a business looks different on its signboard, Instagram and website. We design a logo, colours and type that work together, and give you the files to use them correctly.

For the owner, and for the tech team.

In plain words

A logo, colours and fonts that make your business instantly recognisable, with files and rules so it stays consistent.

Vector logo construction, clear-space and minimum-size rules, colour values for screen and print, type scale, template files and an editable source package.

05 · Questions

Asked before projects like this.

How many logo options do I get?+
We show a small number of considered directions, not dozens. Then we refine the one you choose.
Can you work with my existing logo?+
Yes. Often a cleanup and a proper system is all a business needs.
Do you design social media posts too?+
We design templates for posts as part of the identity, so your posts stay consistent. Ongoing post design can be discussed separately.
