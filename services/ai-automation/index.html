Solution 05 · AI & automation

Automation for the boring work. AI only where it actually helps.

A lot of daily work is repeated: replying, reminding, copying data, making reports. We automate what follows fixed rules, use AI where language is involved, and keep a person in the loop where it matters.

03 · Two ways to explain it

For the owner, and for the tech team.

Your repeated tasks run on their own, and AI helps where it is reliable, with a person checking what matters.

05 · Questions

Asked before projects like this.

Will AI replace my staff?+
That is not the goal. We take repetitive tasks off their plate so they can spend time on customers and decisions.
What if the AI gives a wrong answer?+
We limit it to information you approve, show it uncertain cases for a person to check, and keep logs.
What does it cost to run?+
Some AI and automation tools charge monthly or per use. We list those costs in the proposal before you decide.
