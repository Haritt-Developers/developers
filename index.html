Questions

Asked before every project.

Something else on your mind? Ask it in the First Look form.

Do I need to know exactly what I want?

No. Most clients start with a problem, not a plan. Finding the right solution is our job, and it's the first thing we do.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on what your business needs, so we don't publish a price list. After a short conversation you get a written proposal with a fixed setup cost and an optional monthly care plan. No surprises later.

How long does it take?

A focused business website usually takes 2 to 4 weeks. Apps and custom systems take longer. Your timeline is written in the proposal before we start.

Who will I actually work with?

The founders, directly. No account managers or middlemen, and we work in English or Hindi.

What happens after launch?

30 days of free fixes. After that, an optional care plan covers updates, small changes and a monthly review.

Will I own everything?

Yes. Domain, code, accounts and data are set up in your name from day one.
