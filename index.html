03 · How we work

From tangle to path.

Understand the business. Identify the problem. Build the right technology. Move forward. Small project or large, the order stays the same.

Where you are now

Lots happening, nothing connected.

Calls, chats, spreadsheets, a website that does little. Effort goes in every day, but nothing works together and growth is hard to see.

Step 1 · Understand

We learn how your business runs.

Who your customers are, how work moves today, and what is not working. Before any design or code.

You getRequirement notes
Step 2 · Identify

We find the real problem, and the right fix.

We look at your setup, competitors and options, then recommend what to build, what to skip, the time and the cost.

You getProject brief, proposal and quote
Step 3 · Build

We build the right technology.

Website, app, software or marketing, whatever the problem needs. You see a working preview as it grows, then we launch together.

You getTested, launched product
Step 4 · Move forward

Your business moves to the next stage.

We hand over access and documentation, fix issues and suggest what to improve next, so the growth keeps going.

You getHandover and next steps

05 · How we treat every project

The same approach, whether the project is small or large.

01

We understand before we recommend.

We ask about your business, customers and what is not working before suggesting anything.

02

We recommend only what you need.

If a smaller solution solves the problem, we say so, even when you asked for something bigger.

03

Scope is agreed before we build.

You see what we plan to build, how long it should take and what it costs, in writing, before work begins.

04

You can see the work as it grows.

A preview link to the work in progress, so nothing arrives as a surprise at the end.

05

We explain in plain language.

Choices are explained in words you use. Technical detail is there when you want it.

06

We label our work honestly.

Concept designs are marked as concepts and our own products as our own. We do not invent clients, reviews or numbers.
