01 · What we help you grow

We start with what your business needs to grow. Then we choose the technology.

More customers, more sales, loyal regulars and less time lost to manual work. Open a goal to see what we would build for it.

Goal 01

Get found, and be trusted when they find you.

We buildWebsite, Google Business profile, SEO, brand identity
+

Most customers search before they call. We make sure they find you on Google and Maps, and that your website and brand answer their questions clearly enough to make them reach out.

Goal 02

Turn enquiries and visitors into sales.

We buildOnline store, booking, payments, catalogues
+

Booking, ordering and paying should take a few taps, on any phone. We remove the steps where people give up, and connect payments, stock and delivery so you are not chasing orders by hand.

Goal 03

Keep customers coming back.

We buildWhatsApp and email updates, reminders, loyalty, customer apps
+

Winning a customer once is expensive. Timely reminders, order updates, offers for regulars and an app they actually open keep them engaged long after the first sale.

Goal 04

Run the business with less manual work.

We buildDashboards, automation, AI, custom software
+

Orders, enquiries, staff tasks and reports in one place, with repetitive work done automatically. Built around how your team already works, so it gets used.

Goal 05

Turn an idea into a real product.

We buildWeb and mobile apps, product design
+

We shape the idea into a focused first version, design and build it for web, Android or iPhone, launch it, and improve it from what real users do.

05 · How we treat every project

The same approach, whether the project is small or large.

01

We understand before we recommend.

We ask about your business, customers and what is not working before suggesting anything.

02

We recommend only what you need.

If a smaller solution solves the problem, we say so, even when you asked for something bigger.

03

Scope is agreed before we build.

You see what we plan to build, how long it should take and what it costs, in writing, before work begins.

04

You can see the work as it grows.

A preview link to the work in progress, so nothing arrives as a surprise at the end.

05

We explain in plain language.

Choices are explained in words you use. Technical detail is there when you want it.

06

We label our work honestly.

Concept designs are marked as concepts and our own products as our own. We do not invent clients, reviews or numbers.
