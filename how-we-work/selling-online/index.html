Example · how we think

“I want to sell my products online, but I don’t know how.”

A website agency would quote for an e-commerce site. We start by understanding the business, then set up a complete way of selling online. The website is only one part of it.

Talk about selling onlineSee the nine parts
01 · What it can include

Nine parts of an online selling system.

Not every business needs all nine. We recommend only the parts that fit, and say clearly which ones we build and which ones come from a partner.

  1. 01

    Understand the business

    What you sell, who buys it, where they are, how you sell today and what you want from selling online.

    WhoWe do
  2. 02

    Recommend the right way to start

    Your own store, marketplaces, quick-commerce, social selling, or a mix. For a new product, a marketplace often brings more sales than your own store, and we will say so.

    WhoWe do
  3. 03

    Build the store, if it fits

    An e-commerce website with product pages, cart and checkout, built for phones first.

    WhoWe build
  4. 04

    Payments

    A payment gateway account in your business name, with UPI, cards and COD where needed.

    WhoPartner, we set up
  5. 05

    Marketplaces

    Seller accounts and listings on the marketplaces that suit your product. If you don't know how to list, we create the listings for you.

    WhoPartner, we set up
  6. 06

    Delivery partner

    Courier options compared on rates, COD fees and coverage. You choose, we set it up.

    WhoPartner, we set up
  7. 07

    Being found

    SEO basics, Google Business Profile and the first marketing steps to reach buyers.

    WhoWe do
  8. 08

    Connected

    Orders, stock, payments and customer messages working together, so nothing is typed twice.

    WhoWe build
  9. 09

    Support and improve

    Fixes, updates and next steps as the business grows.

    WhoWe do
The same thinking, any problem

More customers, less manual work, a new app or a first website: we understand the business first, recommend the right mix, and make it happen. We do not promise sales, and we tell you when something is not needed.
