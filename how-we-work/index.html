How we work

From your first message to long after launch.

Small project or large, the order stays the same: understand, discover, strategise, create, improve. You always know what happens next and what you will receive.

Five steps, each with something you can hold.

    You receiveRequirement notes

02 · Pricing

How the price is worked out.

We do not publish a price list, because two businesses rarely need the same thing. Every quote is prepared after we understand the requirement. Share your budget, and we try our best to deliver what you need within it.

What shapes the price

  • How many pages, screens or workflows are needed
  • Features and integrations, such as payments, WhatsApp or a CRM
  • Whether content, design or a brand needs to be created
  • Timeline and level of testing required

What the proposal shows

  • What will be built, and what will not
  • The steps, with an expected timeline
  • The price for the agreed scope
  • Any third-party costs, such as hosting, domains or paid tools, listed separately
