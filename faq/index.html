FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

What businesses usually ask before we start. Not seeing yours? Ask it when you start a conversation.

Do I need to know exactly what I want?+
No. Most clients start with a problem, not a plan. Working out the right solution is the first thing we do.
What do you build?+
Websites, web apps and dashboards, mobile apps, online stores, automation and AI features, and brand identities. See What we build.
How much does a project cost?+
It depends on what your business needs, so we don't publish a price list. After we understand the requirement, you get a written quote for the agreed scope. Share your budget too, and we try our best to deliver within it. See how pricing works.
How long does it take?+
It depends on what is being built. A simple website takes much less time than an app or a custom system. The expected timeline is part of the proposal.
Who will I work with?+
The founders, directly. No account managers or middlemen. We work in English or Hindi.
What do I need to provide?+
Your goals, content (text, photos, logo) and access to your domain or accounts. If you don't have content yet, we help you prepare it.
Can I follow the progress?+
Yes. You get a preview link to the work in progress and a private project page for progress and approvals.
Do you work with businesses outside Jaipur or India?+
Yes. We work remotely, so location is rarely a problem.
