About

We started with websites. Now we help businesses solve what holds them back.

Haritt Developers is the business solutions company of Haritt Group. We help businesses work out what they need, find the right solution and make it happen: through technology we build, services we set up, or both.

01 · Our story

How we got here.

  1. 2020

    A class project

    Haritt began as a project while learning web development at a coaching institute.

  2. 2020–2021

    Haritt Development

    We ran as a small studio making websites for local businesses until July 2021.

  3. 2026

    Haritt Developers

    We restarted as part of Haritt Group, with a wider goal: to be a business solutions partner. Not just websites, but whatever a business needs to solve a problem, including apps, automation, marketing and the right partner services.

02 · What we believe

Three rules we work by.

01

Understand first.

A request for a website is often a different problem in disguise. We find that problem before we build.

02

The right solution, not our favourite one.

We recommend what helps the business, even if it is smaller than what was asked for, or a service someone else provides.

03

Honest work only.

No guarantees we cannot keep, no invented numbers, no fake reviews. We tell you what we build and what a partner provides.
