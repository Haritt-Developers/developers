How do you spell Haritt Developers, and is it the same as Harit Developers?

Haritt Developers is spelled H-A-R-I-T-T, with two Ts. People sometimes type Harit Developers with one T. It is the same company: the digital services brand of Haritt Group.

Who is behind Haritt Developers?

Haritt Developers is a brand of Haritt Group (haritt.com), founded by Harsh Goyal and Pulkit Soni. Haritt Group also makes HV Verse (thehvverse.com), a set of free AI apps.
